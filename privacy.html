The digital twin (AI chat)

The terminal on the home page answers questions about me. When the AI mode is on, the questions you type and the twin's previous replies in the same session (at most the last 12 messages, 600 characters each) are sent through a proxy I run on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare Inc., USA) to the OpenAI API (my contract partner is OpenAI Ireland Ltd., Dublin; the data may also be processed by OpenAI, L.L.C., USA), which generates the answer. To limit abuse, the proxy uses your IP address: it is kept in memory for up to 10 minutes (rate limiting), and a one-way hash of it is stored together with a request counter until the end of the day (00:00 UTC), then deleted. Neither is passed on to OpenAI. I do not keep logs of the conversations.

OpenAI does not use API data to train its models. It may keep requests for up to 30 days to detect abuse, then deletes them. Cloudflare and OpenAI process this data on my behalf under data processing agreements. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, and otherwise on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering visitors' questions about me). Using the chat is optional. Please don't enter personal data about yourself or others in it. The answers are generated by AI and can be wrong. The reliable sources are my CV and my email.
